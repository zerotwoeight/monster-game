Board — Lobby

Host opens /board. Room code, player and spectator URLs, QR code, joined players with starter sets, round-limit picker and Start Game. ⚙ opens sound settings.

Board — Setup phase

Players take turns placing their starting summons for free. The top bar shows setup round and turn; the right rail lists players and the event log.

Board — A normal turn

28-tile wheel with tile art, stationed summon cards, tile numbers and player banners. Centre shows the die; the parchment right rail shows each player's tiles, stationed count, battle record and Mana, then the live event log.

Board — Battles

The attacker card updates live as the player browses their hand; then the clash plays with hit animations, HP bars and ability callouts.

Board — Round income tally

From round 2, each turn starts with the board animating every stationed summon's income (with ability labels), then a compact summary callout.

Board — Wild card event

Banner at the top of the wheel while the player chooses a wild card.

Board — Round callouts

Played before the penultimate and final rounds (with Warning-Sting.mp3; the final round also crossfades to final-round music).

Board — Dropouts — pause & Remove

A disconnect pauses the game. The host can Dismiss, Remove the player (two taps within 4 s), or End Game.

Board — Sound settings

Per-device Music and Sound Effects sliders with mute toggles, saved in the browser.

Board — Game over

Fanfare overlay, then the final standings with awards.

Phone — Join & lobby

Room code, name, starter set (A–F) and an optional Desktop Mode. After joining, the lobby lists everyone.

Phone — Setup

How-to-play walkthrough first, then the board ring: pick an open tile on the ring and a summon from the hand strip; the sheet shows home-field boost or weak ground.

Phone — Waiting & turn start

Between turns the phone shows the whole board as a ring that follows the active player, plus recent events. Your turn starts with the income summary.

Phone — Shop

One action per turn: buy, sell or skip. Tapping a card opens the swipeable detail sheet with the action button.

Phone — Roll & landing choice

Roll 2d6 (min 3), then choose one of three tiles. Each option opens a preview sheet before you commit.

Phone — Claim

Empty tile: pick a summon to station (pay its cost) or skip.

Phone — Battle

Enemy tile: pick an attacker (live preview on the board) or retreat. Matchups show as gold glow (2×) or red tint (½×) with a banner on the sheet. Both players then see a personal result.

Phone — Your own tile

Heal, sacrifice, change element, or swap in a summon from your hand.

Phone — Special tiles

Temple (trade for an Ascendant), Healing Spring and Treasure Chest.

Phone — Stalemate

Next turn after a stalemate: battle again or walk away for 8✦.

Phone — Wild cards

Pick one of three tier cards (or skip), reveal, choose a target. Targeted players get a full-screen notice with a Dismiss button.

Phone — Menu, sound & leaving

☰ in the status bar (or floating ☰ where the status bar is hidden): sound settings, how to play, leave game.

Phone — Eliminated & game over

Phone — Card detail sheet

Tapping any card opens the shared detail sheet: stats, HP, type matchup, ability and lore. Swipe for the next card.

Cards — Card states

One illustrated card per monster, reused everywhere. Type is the coloured frame + label; the stat strip shows HP bar, ATK, DEF, Gen and cost.

All WebSocket messages. Payload shape shown. ⚠ Use exact action names — old names (buy_monster, roll_dice, claim_tile, etc.) are stale and silently ignored.
