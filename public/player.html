How to Play
Element Wheel
Each element deals 2× damage to the next clockwise,
and ½× damage to the one before it.
Overview
✦Collect Mana at the start of every turn — 5 flat, plus your stationed summons' generation.
🛒Shop before rolling. Buy one summon, sell one from your hand, or skip.
🎲Roll to move around the 28-tile board.
⚑Claim empty tiles by deploying a summon from your hand — you pay that summon's Mana Cost.
⚔Battle on enemy tiles. Win to claim them; lose and your summon is destroyed.
✋Your Hand holds up to 5 summons. A summon leaves your hand the moment it's stationed.
Win Condition
There are two ways to win:
🏆Most tiles — the game ends after the round limit (30, 40, or 60 rounds). The player with the most tiles wins. Mana breaks ties.
⚔Last player standing — if you end your turn owning zero tiles (after the first round), you're eliminated. The last active player wins immediately, no need to wait out the rounds.
Tiles compound your income — more tiles means more Mana Gen per turn, which lets you buy stronger summons, which lets you take more tiles. Don't let yourself be wiped out!
Battle
⚔Damage = (ATK − DEF) × type modifier + small roll. Minimum 1.
🏰A summon defending on its own element tile gets +15% ATK & DEF. An element that beats its type costs −15%.
🔄If the defender survives, they counter-strike immediately.
🏳Retreat before attacking to avoid the fight — costs up to 20 Mana penalty. If you have no summons in hand you retreat automatically.
🤝Stalemate — both survive. Next turn you choose to attack again or move on.
Special Tiles
💧Mana Well — bonus Mana whenever you pass or land on it.
⛩Temple — trade a normal summon for an Ascendant (see below).
❤Healing Tile — spend Mana to heal any of your stationed summons.
📦Treasure Chest — random reward: heal potion (45%), battle charm (25%), warp (15%), or a free summon (15%).
Temple Trades
The Temple offers 3 Ascendant summons. To trade:
1You need a matching-element summon in your hand to sacrifice.
2Pay the Ascendant's Mana Cost (always 20✦).
3Your summon is surrendered and the Ascendant is added to your hand.
Each Ascendant has a unique ability and a usability condition that must be met before you can deploy it:
✦Ardent Saint — Aura of Renewal. Condition: healed 4+ summons this game.
✦Succubus — Mana Drain (steal 10✦ on win). Condition: have more Mana than every opponent.
✦Arcane Arbiter — Nullify type modifier once per battle. Condition: changed a tile element 3+ times.
✦Demon Lord — Last Rite (survive at 15 HP once). Condition: had 4+ summons destroyed.
✦Red Dragon — Intimidate (neighbors pay 5✦ on claims). Condition: most summons stationed.
✦World Tree — Deep Roots (+1 Gen per Nature tile owned). Condition: own 4 Nature tiles.
Tips
💡Type advantage is a massive swing — a 2× hit can eliminate most summons in one shot. Always check the element wheel before attacking.
💡Landing on your own tile lets you swap a stationed summon, heal it, or change the tile's element — use this to set up better matchups.
💡A Battle Charm from a Chest gives +25% damage for one fight — assign it to your strongest attacker or a summon about to face a tough defender.
💡Sell summons you can't afford to deploy back at the shop for partial Mana. A damaged summon is worth less, so sell before they take hits.
💡Guardians make great tile defenders. Assailants are cheap attackers that can swing battles on type advantage.
