How to Play
Element Wheel
Each element deals 2× damage to the next clockwise,
and ½× damage to the one before it.
Your Turn
✦Income: from round 2 you collect Mana at the start of your turn. You get 5✦, plus the Gen of each summon you have stationed, minus 1✦ upkeep for each tile you own. At 150✦ or more, your income is halved.
🛒Shop: before you roll you can buy one summon, sell one from your hand, or skip. Your hand holds up to 5 summons.
🎲Roll: roll two dice, then pick one of 3 tiles to land on.
🎴Doubles: if you roll doubles, you get a Wild Card at the end of your turn. There's one face-down card in each tier (Iron 5✦, Arcane 30✦, Gilded 60✦). Buy one without seeing it and turn it over, or skip.
Where You Land
⚑Empty tile: claim it by paying a summon's deploy fee, which is the ✦ on the card. Assailants and Vanguards cost 6✦; Guardians, Ascendants and Support cost 8✦; Rares cost 10✦. The shop still charges the buy price.
🏠Your own tile: do one thing: Swap in a different summon (deploy fee), Heal 20 HP (5✦), Sacrifice the summon for +15✦ (the tile goes neutral), or Change the tile's element (18✦).
⚔Enemy tile: fight or retreat. If you win, you take the tile. If you lose, your summon is destroyed.
🐾Wild monster: nobody owns it. If you beat it, you claim the tile and get +4✦. If you lose, you pay 8✦ and it stays.
Battle
⚔Damage: your ATK minus their DEF, times the type modifier (minimum 1). When Battle Dice is on, both players roll: the black die is the hit you deal and the white die is the hit you take.
🏰Home ground: a defender on a tile of its own element gets +15% ATK and DEF. A defender on a tile whose element beats it gets −15% DEF. Dual-element summons get the bonus if either type matches, and the penalty never applies to them.
🔄Counter-strike: if the defender survives, it strikes back right away.
🏳Retreat: skip the fight for up to 8✦. If you have no summons in your hand, you retreat automatically.
🤝Stalemate: if both summons survive, the attacker loses 4✦ and the defender gains 4✦.
⚔️Contest: if your turn starts on a tile with someone else's summon (you lost or stalemated there, or it was taken from under you), you can Contest it for free: pick a summon and fight, and your turn ends after the fight. Or Skip for free and play your turn as normal. Backing out of a contest costs the normal retreat.
✦Mana swings: if the attacker wins, the attacker gets +4✦ and the defender loses 4✦. If the attacker loses, or both summons are destroyed, the attacker loses 8✦ and the defender gets +4✦.
Special Tiles
💧Mana Well: +40✦.
📦Treasure Chest: a random reward: heal potion (45%), Battle Charm (25%), Warp (15%) or a free summon (15%).
❤Healing Spring: pay 10✦ to heal every summon you have, stationed or in your hand, by 10 HP.
⛩Temple: offer a summon and pay 15✦ to get its element's Ascendant (see Temple Offerings).
Winning
🏆Most tiles: the game ends after the round limit (10, 20 or 30 rounds; setup doesn't count). The player with the most tiles wins, and Mana breaks ties. Active players rank above Sanctioned players, and Sanctioned players rank above eliminated ones.
⚔Last player standing: if every other player is Sanctioned or eliminated, you win right away.
⛓Mana hits zero: you become Sanctioned, but you're not out.
💀No tiles: if you end your own turn with no tiles (after round 1), you're eliminated. A Sanctioned player who loses their last tile is eliminated too.
More tiles means more income, which buys stronger summons, which take more tiles.
Sanctioned
You keep your tiles, summons and hand, but you get no income and you don't shop, roll or move. Your stationed summons still defend, and you still collect defender Mana.
🃏Sanction turn: you're shown 3 random claimed tiles. Pick one or pass.
🔮Rival's tile: put one of 3 Omens on it. Omens are Blessings or Curses that last one round.
🎡Your own tile: Sacrifice the summon for its sell value, Redeem, or flip the Wheel of Fortune.
🌱Redeem: pay 15✦ to rejoin play next turn with +5✦. You can only do this once per game, not in the final round, and you need at least one tile.
⚖Wheel of Fortune: a tarot card moves the tile's Fortune up (Blessing) or down (Curse), up to 3 either way. A flip that lands on the side already at 3 pays a +5✦ jackpot.
Temple Offerings
1Tap a summon to see the Ascendant of its element.
2Pay 15✦. Your summon is given up and the Ascendant goes into your hand.
3Rares, Support cards and Ascendants can't be offered. You can also leave for free.
Each Ascendant needs its condition met before you can deploy it:
✦Ardent Saint: your other stationed summons heal 5 HP each turn. Needs: healed 2+ summons.
✦Succubus: steals 10✦ when it wins an attack or causes a stalemate. Needs: 40✦ or more.
✦Arcane Arbiter: when attacked, it scrambles one of the attacker's tiles. Needs: changed 1+ tile to Arcane.
✦Demon Lord: survives at 15 HP once per game. Needs: 2+ of your summons destroyed in battle.
✦Red Dragon: each enemy summon next to it costs its owner 5✦ every turn. Needs: 3+ summons stationed.
✦World Tree: +1 Gen for each Nature tile and Nature summon you have. Needs: own 2+ Nature tiles.
Tips
💡A 2× type hit can take out most summons in one go, so check the wheel before you attack.
💡Use Sacrifice on your own tile when you need Mana fast.
💡A Battle Charm adds +25% to a summon's next strike. Give it to your best attacker.
💡Damaged summons sell for less, so sell before they take hits.
💡Guardians hold tiles well. Assailants are cheap hitters that win on type advantage.
💡Clever player will notice that tiles claimed through force are free from strings ;)
