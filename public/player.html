How to Play
Element Wheel
Each element deals 2× damage to the next clockwise,
and ½× damage to the one before it.
Overview
✦Collect Mana at the start of every turn from round 2: 5 flat, plus your stationed summons' generation, minus 1✦ upkeep per tile you own. At 150✦ or more, income is halved.
🛒Shop before rolling. Buy one summon, sell one from your hand, or skip.
🎲Roll to move around the 28-tile board.
⚑Claim empty tiles by deploying a summon from your hand: you pay that summon's Mana Cost.
⚔Battle on enemy tiles. Win to claim them; lose and your summon is destroyed.
✋Your Hand holds up to 5 summons. A summon leaves your hand the moment it's stationed.
Win Condition
Two ways to win, and two ways to drop out:
🏆Most tiles: the game ends after the round limit (10, 20 or 30 rounds, chosen in the lobby; setup doesn't count). The player with the most tiles wins. Mana breaks ties. Active players rank above Sanctioned players, who rank above eliminated ones.
⚔Last player standing: if every other player is Sanctioned or eliminated, you win immediately without waiting out the rounds.
⛓Mana hits zero: you're Sanctioned, not out. See Sanctioned below.
💀No tiles: if you end your own turn owning zero tiles (after round 1), you're eliminated. A Sanctioned player who loses their last tile is eliminated too.
Tiles compound your income: more tiles means more Mana Gen per turn, which lets you buy stronger summons, which lets you take more tiles. Don't let yourself be wiped out!
Battle
⚔Damage = (ATK − DEF) × type modifier + small roll. Minimum 1.
🏰A summon defending on its own element tile gets +15% ATK & DEF. On a tile whose element beats it, it gets −15% DEF. Dual-element summons get the bonus if either type matches and are never weakened.
🔄If the defender survives, they counter-strike immediately.
🏳Retreat before attacking to avoid the fight. Costs up to 8✦. If that drops your Mana to zero, you're Sanctioned. If you have no summons in hand you retreat automatically.
🤝Stalemate: both survive. The attacker loses 4✦, the defender gains 4✦. On your next turn, choose Fight again (your turn ends after) or Walk away (pay up to 8✦, then shop and roll as normal).
✦Battle Mana swings: every fight shifts Mana. Attacker wins: +4✦ attacker, −4✦ defender. Attacker loses or mutual: −8✦ attacker, +4✦ defender. Running out of Mana in a battle makes you Sanctioned.
Special Tiles
💧Mana Well: +40 Mana when you land on it.
🎲Doubles: roll doubles and you get a Wild Card at the end of your turn — one card per tier, buy one or skip.
⛩Temple: trade a normal summon for an Ascendant (see below).
❤Healing Spring: pay 10✦ to heal all your summons, stationed and in your hand, +10 HP each.
📦Treasure Chest: random reward: heal potion (45%), battle charm (25%), warp (15%), or a free summon (15%).
Sanctioned
When your Mana hits zero you're Sanctioned. You keep your tiles, summons and hand, but you get no income and you don't shop, roll or move. Your stationed summons still defend, and you still collect the defender's Mana from battles on your tiles.
🃏Sanction turn: you're shown 3 random claimed tiles. Pick one, or pass.
🔮Rival's tile: choose one of 3 Omens to place on it, a Blessing or a Curse that lasts one round.
🎡Your own tile: Sacrifice the summon for its sell value, Redeem, or flip the Wheel of Fortune.
🌱Redeem: pay 15✦ to return to normal play next turn with +5✦. Once per game, not in the final round, and you need at least one tile.
⚖Wheel of Fortune: a tarot card moves the tile's Fortune meter up (Blessing) or down (Curse), up to 3 either way. Blessings boost the summon's DEF and defence rewards; Curses weaken it and pay whoever takes the tile. Flip at the cap for a +5✦ jackpot.
Temple Trades
The Temple offers 3 Ascendant summons. To trade:
1You need a matching-element summon in your hand to sacrifice.
2Pay the Ascendant's Mana Cost (always 20✦).
3Your summon is surrendered and the Ascendant is added to your hand.
Each Ascendant has a unique ability and a usability condition that must be met before you can deploy it:
✦Ardent Saint: Aura of Renewal. Condition: healed 2+ summons this game.
✦Succubus: Mana Drain (steal 10✦ on win). Condition: have at least 40✦ Mana.
✦Arcane Arbiter: Chaos Flux (when attacked, scrambles one of the attacker's tiles). Condition: changed 1+ tile to Arcane this game.
✦Demon Lord: Last Rite (survive at 15 HP once). Condition: had 2+ summons destroyed in battle.
✦Red Dragon: Intimidate (each adjacent enemy summon costs its owner 5✦ every turn). Condition: have 3+ summons stationed on board.
✦World Tree: Deep Roots (+1 Gen per Nature tile owned). Condition: own 2+ Nature tiles.
Tips
💡Type advantage is a massive swing: a 2× hit can eliminate most summons in one shot. Always check the element wheel before attacking.
💡Landing on your own tile lets you heal your stationed summon (5✦ for +20 HP) or sacrifice it for +15✦ Mana and the tile goes neutral. Use the sacrifice when you need Mana fast.
💡A Battle Charm from a Chest gives +25% damage for one fight: assign it to your strongest attacker or a summon about to face a tough defender.
💡Sell summons you can't afford to deploy back at the shop for partial Mana. A damaged summon is worth less, so sell before they take hits.
💡Guardians make great tile defenders. Assailants are cheap attackers that can swing battles on type advantage.
