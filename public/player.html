How to Play
Element Wheel
Each element deals 2× damage to the next clockwise,
and ½× damage to the one before it.
Overview
✦Collect Mana at the start of every turn — 5 flat, plus your stationed monsters' generation.
🛒Shop before rolling. Buy one monster, sell one from your hand, or skip.
🎲Roll to move around the 28-tile board.
⚑Claim empty tiles by deploying a monster from your hand — you pay that monster's Mana Cost.
⚔Battle on enemy tiles. Win to claim them; lose and your monster is destroyed.
✋Your Hand holds up to 5 monsters. A monster leaves your hand the moment it's stationed.
Win Condition
The game ends after the round limit (30, 40, or 60 rounds). The player with the most tiles wins. If there's a tie, the player with more Mana takes it.
Tiles compound your income — more tiles means more Mana Gen per turn, which lets you buy stronger monsters, which lets you take more tiles.
Battle
⚔Damage = (ATK − DEF) × type modifier + small roll. Minimum 1.
🏰A monster defending on its own element tile gets +15% ATK & DEF. An element that beats its type costs −15%.
🔄If the defender survives, they counter-strike immediately.
🏳Retreat before attacking to avoid the fight — costs up to 20 Mana penalty. If you have no monsters in hand you retreat automatically.
🤝Stalemate — both survive. Next turn you choose to attack again or move on.
Special Tiles
💧Mana Well — bonus Mana whenever you pass or land on it.
⛩Temple — trade a normal monster for an Ascendant (see below).
❤Healing Tile — spend Mana to heal any of your stationed monsters.
📦Treasure Chest — random reward: heal potion (45%), battle charm (25%), warp (15%), or a free monster (15%).
Temple Trades
The Temple offers 3 Ascendant monsters. To trade:
1You need a matching-element monster in your hand to sacrifice.
2Pay the Ascendant's Mana Cost (always 20✦).
3Your monster is surrendered and the Ascendant is added to your hand.
Each Ascendant has a unique ability and a usability condition that must be met before you can deploy it:
✦Ardent Saint — Aura of Renewal. Condition: healed 4+ monsters this game.
✦Succubus — Mana Drain (steal 10✦ on win). Condition: have more Mana than every opponent.
✦Arcane Arbiter — Nullify type modifier once per battle. Condition: changed a tile element 3+ times.
✦Demon Lord — Last Rite (survive at 15 HP once). Condition: had 4+ monsters destroyed.
✦Red Dragon — Intimidate (neighbors pay 5✦ on claims). Condition: most monsters stationed.
✦World Tree — Deep Roots (+1 Gen per Nature tile owned). Condition: own 4 Nature tiles.
Tips
💡Type advantage is a massive swing — a 2× hit can eliminate most monsters in one shot. Always check the element wheel before attacking.
💡Landing on your own tile lets you swap a stationed monster, heal it, or change the tile's element — use this to set up better matchups.
💡A Battle Charm from a Chest gives +25% damage for one fight — assign it to your strongest attacker or a monster about to face a tough defender.
💡Sell monsters you can't afford to deploy back at the shop for partial Mana. A damaged monster is worth less, so sell before they take hits.
💡Guardians make great tile defenders. Assailants are cheap attackers that can swing battles on type advantage.
