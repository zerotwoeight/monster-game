Wild Cards
Triggered when a player passes the Mana Well (position 0). One card drawn per tier — player picks or skips.
State 1 — Tier Masks (card-back shown before player picks)
When the Wild Card event triggers, the player sees 3 face-down cards — one per tier. The card back reveals the tier (Iron / Arcane / Gilded) and cost, but hides the card name and effect until selected. Each tier needs one mask/back-face image.
State 2 — Card Art (revealed art for each individual card)
After a player selects a card (or during the effect description), the full card art is shown. Each of the 12 cards needs unique art. File naming: public/img/wildcards/wc-[id].png

Dual-type summons — strong against two types, no elemental weakness. Obtained via Warp or special events. Never in the shop.
